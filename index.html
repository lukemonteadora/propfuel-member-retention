Common questions

Things people ask before they book.

How is this different from our email tool?

Email tools send messages. PropFuel starts conversations. Every message captures an answer from the member and writes it back to your AMS automatically. You stop guessing what members want, because they tell you.

Will this replace our AMS?

No. PropFuel works with the association management system you already have, including Nimble AMS, Fonteva, iMIS, YourMembership and MemberClicks. It reads member data and writes engagement answers back. Your AMS stays your system of record.

How much work is this for my team?

Under three hours in total to get started. Connecting your AMS takes 5 to 30 minutes and training takes 10 to 45 minutes. After that, your team approves campaigns and watches results. If you can send an email, you can use PropFuel.

What happens to our member data?

Your data is yours. Member answers are stored securely and written back to your AMS. Your data is never used to train AI models, and Membership AI requests exclude personally identifiable information.

What does it cost after the free campaign?

PropFuel is an annual subscription based on your active membership size, plus a one-time setup fee. Every plan includes unlimited users, campaigns, emails and contacts. Your rate is locked at signing, and you get an exact quote on the call. There is no obligation after the free campaign.

What size association is this for?

PropFuel works with associations from about 5,000 members to 500,000+. Pricing scales with your membership size, so it fits whether you have a team of two or twenty.
